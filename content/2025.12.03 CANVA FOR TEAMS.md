# 🧭 CraftLit Canva Team Setup

This guide has two sections:

- **Left Column:** Context & Rationale — the “why” behind each choice, so you remember what problem it solved.  
- **Right Column:** Quick Steps — minimal instructions to act fast.

---

## 🧩 1. Confirm You’re Using the Same Canva Workspace

### Left — Why
Canva now organizes everything under **Workspaces**.  
If even one teammate works in a different workspace (like “Personal”), their uploads, templates, and brand kits live in a completely separate cloud container — invisible to the rest of the team.  
Consolidating under one shared **CraftLit Workspace** ensures every file, folder, and brand element lives together.

### Right — Quick Steps
1. Go to [Canva Home](https://www.canva.com/).  
2. In Canva, click your **profile picture** (bottom-left corner).
3. In the pop-up menu, look under **Teams**.
4. Make sure everyone is working in the same team — for you, that’s
    **teamcraftlit** (as shown in your screenshot).
5. If anyone’s creating designs under their personal account instead, have them switch by clicking their profile → selecting **teamcraftlit** before designing or uploading assets.

## 🎨 2. Centralize Shared Assets in Brand Hub

### Left — Why
Brand Hub (a Canva Pro feature) is your single source of truth: it stores fonts, colors, logos, and reusable assets.  
Using this prevents small color or font inconsistencies across thumbnails, YouTube covers, and episode cards.

### Right — Quick Steps
1. Go to **Home → Brand → Brand Hub.**  
2. Create/open your **CraftLit Brand Kit**.  
3. Upload:
   - Logos (SVG/PNG)
   - Fonts
   - Brand colors (hex codes)
   - Backgrounds or recurring images  
4. Now anyone can access it from **Editor → Brand → Your Brand Kit**.

---

## 📁 3. Build a Shared Folder Structure

### Left — Why
Folders in the *Projects* area are the shared “drives” for your Canva workspace.  
Without them, each member’s uploads sit in private storage, creating duplicates and missing assets.

### Right — Quick Steps
1. Go to **Home → Projects → + New Folder.**  
2. Create:
   - `CraftLit – Master Art`
   - `CraftLit – Book Covers`
   - `CraftLit – Social Templates`
   - `CraftLit – Seasonal Campaigns`  
3. Click the **⋯ (three dots)** → **Share** → invite your team.  
4. Give edit rights to designers; view-only to assistants.  
5. Upload *only* to these folders going forward.

---

## 🔄 4. Consolidate Existing Files

### Left — Why
Designs and images often scatter across personal spaces.  
Moving them into shared folders gives everyone a single place to find official assets and avoids orphaned versions.

### Right — Quick Steps
1. Go to **Projects → Your Designs.**  
2. Select the items you own.  
3. Click **Move to Folder → choose shared folder.**  
4. Use filters to find files by creator for cleanup.
